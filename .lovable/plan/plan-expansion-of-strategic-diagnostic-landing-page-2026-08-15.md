# Plan: Expansion of Strategic Diagnostic Landing Page

The objective is to significantly expand the "Consultoria" landing page (strategic diagnostic) to provide deep methodology details, transforming it into a high-fidelity, professional presentation of a premium service.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. Hero Expansion
- Update copy to match the new strategic messaging.
- Reinforce the value proposition: 4 meetings, parallel investigation, customer experience analysis.

### 2. New Conceptual Section: Perception vs Reality
- Add a new section highlighting the gap between operational perception and reality.
- Visual highlight: "PERCEPÇÃO ≠ REALIDADE".

### 3. Expanded Methodology (How it works)
- Expand the 5-step flow (Coleta, Investigação, Experiência, Cruzamento, Diagnóstico).
- Add detailed objectives and descriptions for each stage to avoid superficiality.

### 4. Detailed "Four Meetings" Breakdown
- Convert the existing cards into deep-dive sections.
- Detail the specific items investigated in each meeting (Business, Operation, Management/Tech, Validation).
- Add "Central Question" and "Objective" for each meeting.

### 5. New Section: "Behind the Scenes" Investigation
- Highlight what happens outside of meetings (Data analysis, CRM analysis, process mapping).
- Emphasize the connection between information gathering and strategic synthesis.

### 6. New Section: Customer Experience (Hidden Client)
- Explain the methodology for evaluating the commercial journey from the client's perspective.
- Visual comparison: Internal Process vs Real Experience.

### 7. New Section: Perspective Intersection
- Visual composition showing the intersection of Management, Team, Data, and Client views.
- Focus on the "Tenno Diagnostic" as the convergence point.

### 8. New Section: Strategic Findings
- Define four key areas: Forces, Weaknesses, Bottlenecks, and Opportunities.
- Emphasize understanding over just problem-solving.

### 9. New Section: Investment Roadmap
- Strategic advice on where to invest: People, Processes, Management, Technology, Automation, or Acquisition.

### 10. New Section: The Final Report & Roadmap
- Large visual section detailing the report's contents.
- Introduction of the "Now, Next, Evolution, Scale" roadmap framework.

### 11. Final CTA & "Who it's for"
- Explicitly define the target audience and who is not a fit.
- Reconstruct the final CTA section to be premium and high-impact.

## Technical Details

### Visual Style
- Maintain existing "Questrial" font and pink (#ff0a54) accents.
- Use full-width layouts with generous white space.
- Alternating light/dark sections for rhythm.
- Use glass-morphism blocks for key strategic information.
- Responsive design for all screen sizes.

### File Structure
- Primary updates will be in `src/routes/solucoes.consultoria.tsx`.
- No new external components needed, will use clean semantic HTML and Tailwind.
